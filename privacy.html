Your account and stash

When you create an account, we store your name, email, password (scrambled, never as plain text), boards, saved items and any photos you add. They are stored with our database provider, Supabase, so your stash is there on any device you sign in on. Only you can see your boards and items. See Supabase's privacy policy.

Photos you add yourself are stored under a random link that isn't listed anywhere, but anyone who has that exact link could open it. Don't add photos you want to keep private.

You can delete your account, stash and photos at any time from the account menu.

Reading product links

When you save a link, Stashd asks the store's own website for the product details. If that doesn't work, it may send the link to Microlink (microlink.io), a link-preview service, to get the title and photo. Only the link is sent, not your account details. See Microlink's privacy policy.

News emails (optional)

If you tick the box to get Stashd news, your email address is sent to our email provider, Brevo (Sendinblue SAS), and used only to email you about Stashd's launch and product updates. We do not sell it. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email. See Brevo's privacy policy.
