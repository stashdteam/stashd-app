How it works

Three taps from "ooh" to "it's mine."

Like it

See something in an Instagram or TikTok ad, or on a store's site? Tap Share and pick Stashd, or paste the link.

Stash it

Stashd fills in the photo, title, store and price for you. Drop it into a board like "Summer" or "Eid outfits".

Buy it

When you're ready, open the store page and buy it. Then mark it as bought and watch your "left to spend" total drop.

What's in the first version

Small app, big wishlist energy.

Save from anywhere

Use the share menu on iPhone and Android, or paste a link. Any store, any ad.

Details fill themselves in

Photo, title, store, price, the old price and whether it's sold out. No typing.

Boards you'll actually use

Sort items into boards and scroll them in a two-column photo grid.

To buy, bought, left to spend

Two tabs keep wants and haves apart, and a running total shows what's still to spend.

Tags at a glance

Cards show when something is sold out and how much is off.

Everything about one item

Photo, price, size, a note, its board, a sold-out switch, Mark as bought and a link back to the store.
